## 🚀 Main Projects

### [VerifyLive](https://github.com/adriaansaeez/verifylive)
Live fact-checking for Spanish speech: transcribes a debate, rally or interview **in your own browser** with WebGPU, searches the press and official sources, and shows in real time what is backed, what is missing context and what simply cannot be checked. Every verdict ships with the sources it was taken from, and the audio never leaves your machine. `Next.js` `WebGPU` `TypeSafe Jev` `SearXNG`

### [Organic Story Engine](https://github.com/adriaansaeez/organic-story-engine)
A kit for running a short-form series your audience lives in: followers become characters, comments become scenes, and every episode ends with a vote that changes the canon. The machine handles pixel art, maps, sound and timing; people decide who is in the story and what they ask for. Every workflow is a Claude Code skill. `Python` `Claude Code` `HyperFrames` `ElevenLabs`

### [FitAI](https://github.com/adriaansaeez/FitAI)
Simulated 10-minute race demo where **Jev** (TypeSafe System One) reads the athlete's training state second by second and decides between pacing adjustments — with a deterministic safety layer that always dominates the model's output. Fictional seeded data, no medical advice. `Next.js` `TypeScript` `TypeSafe`
